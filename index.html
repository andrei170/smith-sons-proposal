The smaller jobs, and where the ads point

You mentioned you also want more of the smaller jobs, and you're sending a few photos so I can see exactly what you mean. That matters for how the ads are built, because a small job and a full re-roof are two completely different customers.

The smaller work is what fills the quiet gaps. Leaks, slipped tiles, a bit of guttering, a chimney repoint, they come in fast, they decide fast, and they keep the lads busy between the big jobs. The re-roofs and commercial work are the bigger tickets that are worth chasing harder but land less often. A good ads setup runs both, weighted so the quick jobs keep the diary full while the big ones are worth the wait.

Once I've seen your photos I'll know which small jobs you actually want more of, and which you'd rather leave, so the ads bring in the right ones rather than just more of everything.

How the ads actually work

A leak, a slipped tile and a full re-roof are three completely different customers. One "roofing Leicester" campaign for all of them wastes money on each, so it's built as three, each pointed at its own page on your new site.
