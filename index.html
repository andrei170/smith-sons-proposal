Three things that get done right on the rebuild

These are the faults on the current site you're locked out of. On the new one, in your name, they simply don't exist from the start.

Fix 1 · Biggest

No title or description tags

The site has no page title and no meta description anywhere. That's the line Google shows in search results, so right now there's nothing for it to display. It also pushes up what you'd pay per click on ads, because Google scores landing page quality.

Fix 2

The numbers don't match

The site says 15+ team members, 10,000+ projects and 9,000+ clients. You told me you've got 6. These almost certainly came with the template, but they need correcting before paid traffic lands on them.

Fix 3 · Quickest

"Send Massage"

The button on your contact form says Send Massage instead of Send Message. It's on the exact button someone presses to become a customer. Thirty second fix.

The full rebuild, on your own account, is included in month one at no extra cost. No setup fee, no build charge. If you stopped after month one, the website is yours, on your account, and it stays live. You'd be in a far better spot than you are now, which is locked out of a site that could vanish.

The smaller jobs, and where the ads point

You mentioned you also want more of the smaller jobs, and you're sending a few photos so I can see exactly what you mean. That matters for how the ads are built, because a small job and a full re-roof are two completely different customers.

The smaller work is what fills the quiet gaps. Leaks, slipped tiles, a bit of guttering, a chimney repoint, they come in fast, they decide fast, and they keep the lads busy between the big jobs. The re-roofs and commercial work are the bigger tickets that are worth chasing harder but land less often. A good ads setup runs both, weighted so the quick jobs keep the diary full while the big ones are worth the wait.

Once I've seen your photos I'll know which small jobs you actually want more of, and which you'd rather leave, so the ads bring in the right ones rather than just more of everything.

Being straight with you

There's no guarantee, and I'm not going to pretend otherwise. I can't promise you a number of jobs a week, and anyone who does before running a single ad is guessing. What I can promise is that you'll see exactly what's happening from week one, and that I'll tell you if it isn't working rather than waiting for you to ask.

The one number I need from you: how many jobs you're running a week now, and what six lads could comfortably handle. Everything else I've worked out from your site, but that one has to come from you, and it's what turns this from a general chat into a specific plan.

Also useful: your rough average job value, split between small jobs and full re-roofs. That tells us what an enquiry is genuinely worth to you, which decides how hard we can afford to bid, and your photos will help me see which small jobs you want more of.

Some good news I've already checked for you. Your domain, smithandsonsroofing.uk, is registered through Cloudflare and runs on Cloudflare's system, which is separate from the GoHighLevel account your site sits in.
